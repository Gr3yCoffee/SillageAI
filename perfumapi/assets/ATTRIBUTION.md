Dataset source: [Scento-com/perfume-dataset](https://github.com/Scento-com/perfume-dataset). The snapshot contains 1,479 data rows and 1,473 distinct fragrance URLs after de-duplication; fields that are not verified in the source are left empty. Longevity and projection are not included in this dataset.
